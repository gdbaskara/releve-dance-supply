## Passed (with evidence)

- **R-17 / R-18 / R-36 / R-38:** no statistics, testimonials or ratings in the copy. The demo mode is labelled ("Mode demo, pembayaran & pengiriman disimulasikan"). "Original" claims come from the owner's brief, and the store states it is independent.
- **R-37 / R-06:** a design direction exists in `docs/07-design-system.md`. It gives a reason for the uppercase tracked labels (borrowed from the Só Dança feel) and the two typefaces.
- **R-24 / R-26:** no `href="#"` links. Every nav item routes to a real page.
- **R-03 overflow:** there is no horizontal scroll at 375 px and 320 px (checked in PR #4 and again here).
- **R-39 page-level:** `<html lang>` is set per locale, and zoom is not blocked.
- **R-19 / R-39 motion:** `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected in `globals.css`, spinner and skeleton.
- **R-25 other tokens:** `ink-muted` (7.0), success (4.7), warning (5.3), error (5.6), info (5.5) and `accent-ink` on `accent` (6.6) pass. Input borders use `border-strong` (3.7, above 3:1 for non-text).
